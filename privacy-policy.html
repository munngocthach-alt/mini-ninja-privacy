Privacy Policy

Mini Ninja (Android, iPhone/iPad and Web)
Developer: Thach Nguyen
Contact: mun.ngocthach@gmail.com
Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information Mini Ninja (“the game”) handles on Android, iOS and the Web, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • Mini Ninja has no accounts. It never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • The game is played entirely against the computer. It has no online play, no advertising and no analytics, and it sends no information over the Internet.
  • Your scores, settings and any unfinished match are saved only on your device (or, on the Web, in your browser). The developer never receives them.

2. Information stored on your device

WhatWhyWhere
Score totals (Team Battle, Survival)Achievements screenYour device / browser storage only

You can erase all of it at any time with Settings › Privacy › Delete My Data, or by uninstalling the game (on the Web: by clearing the site's data in your browser).

3. Web version

The Web version (for example on CrazyGames) stores your progress only in your browser. The hosting website may process data, including showing its own ads, under its own policy (CrazyGames: https://www.crazygames.com/privacy-policy).

4. App stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store may collect information when you download or update the game, under their own privacy policies. The developer receives only aggregated statistics from them, such as install counts and, if the store provides them, anonymous crash reports.

5. Children

Mini Ninja does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights over your personal information. Because the game collects none and stores everything on your device, you can exercise control directly with Delete My Data. For questions, contact mun.ngocthach@gmail.com.

7. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
