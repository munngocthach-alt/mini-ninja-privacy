Privacy Policy

Mini Ninja (Android, iPhone/iPad and Web)
Developer: Thach Nguyen
Contact: mun.ngocthach@gmail.com
Effective date: 26 October 2026 (replaces the version of 25 October 2026)

This policy explains what information Mini Ninja (“the game”) handles on Android, iOS and the Web, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • Mini Ninja has no accounts. It never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • The game is played entirely against the computer. It has no online play and no analytics.
  • The Android and iOS apps show an ad after each match, provided by Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with. To deliver those ads, the ad partners receive information about your device, as described in section 3. Ads are personalised only if you agree.
  • The Web version shows no ads of its own and sends nothing to the developer.
  • Your scores, settings and any unfinished match are saved only on your device (or, on the Web, in your browser). The developer never receives them.

3. Advertising (Android and iOS apps)

Who: ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies, and the advertising networks LevelPlay works with on our behalf. Their privacy policies apply to the data they process: Unity's is at https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy, and it explains how to contact Unity and exercise your rights with them.

What the ad partners receive when the app loads or shows an ad:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android; on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking when iOS asks);
  • device and connection information, such as IP address (from which an approximate location such as the country can be derived), device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, and network type;
  • app information, such as the app's name and version;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether you tapped them, and diagnostic data about ad loading and errors.

Why: to choose and show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and report it, detect fraud and abuse, and keep the ad service working. If you agree, it is also used to personalise ads, choosing ads based on your interests inferred from your activity across apps and websites.

The developer does not receive this information about you individually. The developer only sees aggregated reports from LevelPlay, such as how many ads were shown and the revenue earned.

Your choices:

  • First launch: the app asks whether ads may be personalised. Until you answer, and if you choose Not Personalised, the app tells the ad partners that you do not consent to personalised advertising and opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your personal information. Ads are then chosen without your activity in other apps and websites. They still use the device and ad information above to show and measure ads and prevent fraud.
  • Any time: change your choice with the Personalised Ads switch in Settings › Privacy. It applies to ads loaded from then on.
  • iPhone and iPad: if you choose personalised ads, iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you do not allow it, ads stay non-personalised and the IDFA is not shared. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings › Privacy › Ads (the wording varies by device).

4. Web version

The Web version (for example on CrazyGames) shows no ads of its own and stores your progress only in your browser. The hosting website may process data, including showing its own ads, under its own policy (CrazyGames: https://www.crazygames.com/privacy-policy).

5. App stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store may collect information when you download or update the game, under their own privacy policies. The developer receives only aggregated statistics from them, such as install counts and, if the store provides them, anonymous crash reports.

6. Children

Mini Ninja is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and their information reached an ad partner, contact us and we will help you request deletion.

7. Retention and your rights

The developer stores nothing about you outside your device. The ad partners keep data for the periods set out in their own policies.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information. These include the right to access, delete or correct it, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising. Such rights exist, for example, under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, and under the CCPA/CPRA in California.

  • To withdraw consent or opt out, choose Not Personalised (Settings › Privacy) and, on iOS, turn tracking off.
  • To have data deleted or accessed by an ad partner, use the contact routes in that partner's policy (Unity: link above).
  • For anything else, or if you need help, contact mun.ngocthach@gmail.com.

8. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted at this address with an updated effective date. If a change affects your ad choice, the app will ask you again.
