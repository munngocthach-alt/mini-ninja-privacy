Ninja Academy: Shuriken Arena (Android and Web), formerly Mini Ninja
Developer: Thach Nguyen

This policy explains what information Ninja Academy: Shuriken Arena (the "game", formerly Mini Ninja) handles on Android and on the Web, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • The game has no accounts. It never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • Solo modes are played entirely against the computer. The Android app can also be played online with a friend using a room code (section 5). The game has no chat and no analytics.
  • The Android app offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one, and it pays coins. They are provided by Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with. To deliver those ads, the ad partners receive information about your device, as described in section 3. Ads are personalised only if you agree.
  • The Android app sells optional coin packs through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and the developer never sees your payment details (section 4).
  • The Web version shows no ads and sells nothing of its own, and sends nothing to the developer.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device (on the Web, in your browser). There are no cloud saves, and the developer never receives them.

3. Advertising (Android app)

Who: ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies, and the advertising networks LevelPlay works with on our behalf. Their privacy policies apply to the data they process. Unity's is at unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

When: an ad plays only when you choose to watch one for coins, up to 5 a day. The game shows no other ads.

What the ad partners receive when the app loads or shows an ad:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID);
  • device and connection information, such as IP address (from which an approximate location such as the country can be derived), device model, operating system and version, language, time zone and network type;
  • app information, such as the app's name and version;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether you tapped them, and diagnostic data about ad loading and errors.

Why: to choose and show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and report it, detect fraud and abuse, and keep the ad service working. If you agree, it is also used to personalise ads, choosing ads based on your interests inferred from your activity across apps.

The developer does not receive this information about you individually. The developer only sees aggregated reports from LevelPlay, such as how many ads were shown and the revenue earned.

Your choices:

  • First launch: the app asks whether ads may be personalised. Until you answer, and if you choose Not Personalised, the app tells the ad partners that you do not consent to personalised advertising and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information.
  • Any time: change your choice with the Personalised Ads switch in Settings › Privacy. It applies to ads loaded from then on.
  • Not watching: rewarded ads are optional. If you never start one, no ad is shown.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings › Privacy › Ads (the wording varies by device).

4. Coin packs (Android app)

Coin packs are bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The game receives only a confirmation of the order, which it uses to add the coins on your device. The developer receives sales reports from Google and does not receive your card details.

Coins are stored only on your device. If you uninstall the game, use Delete My Data or change device, they are lost.

5. Playing with a friend (Android app)

This applies only if you create or join a room. If you never do, none of it happens.

Who: online play uses Unity's multiplayer services (Unity Technologies), under Unity's privacy policy at unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

What is processed:

  • an anonymous player ID, created by Unity when you first open a room and kept on your device. It isn't linked to your name or any account;
  • your IP address, used to connect you and to work out your approximate location, so a nearby server can be chosen;
  • diagnostic and performance data about the connection;
  • the match data passed between the two devices while you play, such as moves and the state of the match. It passes through Unity's relay servers, and the developer doesn't store it.

Who sees what: your friend sees your chosen character and the game's result. The connection goes through a relay, so your friend's device doesn't see your IP address. There is no chat, no profile and no public list of rooms. Anyone who has your room code can try to join until the room is full.

Why: to connect the two devices and keep the service working and secure.

Deleting it: Delete My Data also asks Unity to delete your player ID, if the device is online at the time. Otherwise the ID is removed from your device. Rooms end when the host leaves.

6. Web version

The Web version (for example on CrazyGames) has no online play, shows no ads of its own and stores your progress only in your browser. It tells the hosting website when loading and play start and stop, which the site's own software uses. The hosting website may process data, including showing its own ads, under its own policy (CrazyGames: www.crazygames.com/privacy-policy).

7. App stores

Google Play may collect information when you download or update the game, under its own privacy policy. The developer receives only aggregated statistics from it, such as install counts and, if the store provides them, anonymous crash reports.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and their information reached an ad or service partner, contact us and we will help you request its deletion.

9. Retention and your rights

The developer stores nothing about you outside your device. The ad partners, Unity and Google keep data for the periods set out in their own policies.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information. These include the right to access, delete or correct it, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Because the developer holds no personal information about you, these requests are handled as follows:

  • To withdraw consent or opt out, choose Not Personalised in Settings › Privacy.
  • To have data deleted or accessed by an ad or service partner, use the contact routes in that partner's policy (Unity: link above).
  • For anything else, or if you need help, contact us at mun.ngocthach@gmail.com.

10. Changes
