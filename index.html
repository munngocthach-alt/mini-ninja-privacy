Ninja Academy: Shuriken Arena

Bouncing shuriken battles, solo or online with a friend. By Thach Nguyen.

Team up with an AI partner or a friend (Android) in 2 vs 2 tournaments, outlast three other teams in Survival, hold off waves of monsters in Endless, or plan your trick shots in turn-based Ricochet. The game has no accounts. The Android app offers optional ads that pay coins and optional coin packs. The Web version has neither.

Privacy

The game keeps your progress only on your device. In the Android app, the ad service receives device information to show the ads you choose to watch, which are personalised only if you agree. Read the privacy policy.
