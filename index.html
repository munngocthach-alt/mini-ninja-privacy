Mini Ninja

Bouncing shuriken battles against the computer. By Thach Nguyen.

Take on an AI team of 1, 2 or 3 in Team Battle, or outlast seven ninjas in Survival, on Normal or Hard. The game is single-player, works offline, and has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.
