Mini Ninja

Bouncing shuriken battles against the computer. By Thach Nguyen.

Take on an AI team of 1, 2 or 3 in Team Battle, or outlast seven ninjas in Survival, on Easy, Normal or Hard. The game is single-player and has no accounts and no in-app purchases. The Android and iPhone apps show an ad after each match; the Web version has none.

Privacy

The game itself keeps your progress only on your device. In the mobile apps, the ad service receives device information to show ads, which are personalised only if you agree. Read the privacy policy.
